# Where a scene is drawn inside a slot: the fit scale, and the offset from the slot's top-left.
#
# `<sceneW>x<sceneH> in <slotW>x<slotH>` and then `scale | left | top`, to six places.
#
# Every case is chosen to come out exact in binary floating point. `CanonicalCalls` records why that
# matters between two languages — Java rounds a tie up and C rounds it to even — and the cheapest
# answer is a golden with no ties in it rather than a rounding routine written twice.
#
# The three renderers each compute this, and disagreed until #99 in 0.3.0: the Android view
# pinned a scene to the padded top-left where the other two centred it, so the same chart in the
# same slot sat in a different place depending on the host. Nothing compared them.
200x100 in 400x400 -> 2.000000 | 0.000000 | 100.000000
100x100 in 400x200 -> 2.000000 | 100.000000 | 0.000000
200x100 in 200x100 -> 1.000000 | 0.000000 | 0.000000
400x200 in 200x100 -> 0.500000 | 0.000000 | 0.000000
100x200 in 400x400 -> 2.000000 | 100.000000 | 0.000000
